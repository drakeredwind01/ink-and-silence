# Possible Names

Names generated during naming brainstorm for the job-application autofill project (sibling to InformRoot). Not used there — banked here for whatever future project fits.

## Portalign

**Mechanism:** Portmanteau with zero filler letters, plus hidden words.

Fuses *Portal* and *Align* seamlessly, sharing the "al." Also contains *Port* (the destination/gate) and *Align* (a pattern-matching mechanism mapping fields to a profile).

Reads as an enterprise-grade utility tool — precise, no fluff, describes exactly what a matching/adapter engine does under the hood.

## EntryWay

**Mechanism:** Double literal reading.

- Reading 1: An "entryway" is a portal or door.
- Reading 2: *Entry* (data entry / entering a competition) + *Way* (the smart path/method an algorithm uses to map fields).

Most welcoming and classic-sounding of the three. Leans on the literal "portal" image while sneaking in the data-entry mechanism.
